Nude For You

a confession, for the day I was born and the day you found me

Tonight the clock forgets to turn,
tonight the candles wait to burn,
tonight I do not wish for years —
I wish to hand you all my fears.

So take me, love, before I speak,
past strong, past sure, past brave, past sleek.
Unmake the man the world has seen,
and meet the one who's always been.

Take off the shirt of who I show,
the practiced calm, the steady flow —
beneath it beats a rawer thing,
a storm that only you can bring.

Take off the zip of guarded pride,
that kept my trembling hands to my side.
I've worn control like second skin —
tonight I let you wander in.

Pull off the ego, thread by thread,
the crown I built, the lines I said,
the man who conquered, closed, contained —
strip him until just I remain.

Take my breath, still warm from your name,
take every hush I couldn't claim,
take the nights I lay awake
just tracing shapes your absence makes.

Take my hands — they know your skin
better than they know my sin.
Take my mouth — it's learned to pray
in every language your lips play.

Take my chest, where thunder hides,
where longing rises with the tides,
where every heartbeat, wild and bare,
has always, only, called you there.

I have to tell you something true — before you, I did not know I was cold until I felt how warm your knowing was. I did not know I'd been holding my breath for years, quietly, politely, calling it fine, until you said my name and something in my chest finally exhaled.

You didn't just find me. You found the parts I'd hidden even from prayer, and you held them like they weren't too much, like they were simply mine, simply human, simply worth keeping. No one had ever done that before. Not like this. Not so gently.

My secrets? Yours. I hide no more.
My locks undone, my hinges, my whole floor
laid open like a house in spring —
come in, take everything.

Your wishes — I will wear like law.
Your every whim, your slightest flaw,
your midnight moods, your morning fire —
I do not calm them. I require
to be the thing that lets them burn,
to be the door through which you turn
when the world gets loud and cold and wrong —
I want to be your only song.

Your desires, I don't just permit —
I chase them down, I welcome it.
Whatever hungers in your chest,
let my whole self become its rest.

Take my past — the wars, the scars,
the nights I built myself from shards.
Take my future — still unwritten,
save one line: that you're within it.

No mask, no armor, no disguise,
just breath and pulse beneath your eyes.
No walls to guard, no gates to climb —
just skin, just soul, undone, sublime.

I am not always brave. I want you to know that. Some days I flinch before I trust. Some days the old fear still knocks on the door and asks if it can stay. But I let you in anyway — every time — because loving you has taught me that softness is not the opposite of strength. It might be the truest form of it.

So take me whole — not part, not piece,
take war-torn calm, take fierce release,
take quiet nights and burning noon,
take every version of me — soon,

and slower still, and closer yet,
until there's nothing left to forget,
until my name means only this:
hers — in a breath, in a kiss.

This is no gift wrapped soft and neat.
This is a man laid at your feet —
not broken down, but broken open,
every silence finally spoken.

And if you ever doubt how deep this runs — remember this night, remember these words, remember that on the one day meant for me, I gave it to you instead, because somewhere along the way, you stopped being someone I love and became the place I come home to.

So here I stand, no seams, no lock,
undone by love, not by shock —
bare to the bone, and still not through,
forever, fully — nude for you.
